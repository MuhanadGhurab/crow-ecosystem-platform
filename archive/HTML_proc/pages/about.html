🛡️About CyberCrow

🔐ERP secured from day one

CyberCrow treats cybersecurity as part of the ERP design—not a late add-on. You move from configuration requests through CyberCrow layers (Crow Shield, Crow Sentinel, Crow Fortress) into subscription and operations with governance-friendly clarity.

🔐

Why embed security?

Permissions, audit trails, and password policy shape the ERP footprint. CyberCrow forces an explicit security choice in the request path instead of hiding it in the last slide of a proposal.

🏢

Who is this for?

Operations, IT, and finance teams that need a crisp ERP scope plus a CyberCrow security tier aligned with risk and compliance expectations.

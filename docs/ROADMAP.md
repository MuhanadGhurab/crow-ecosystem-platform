# Public roadmap

**SecDevOps · AI · Multi-tenant** — high-level direction for this GitHub portfolio.  
Crow Ecosystem is the multi-tenant proof; percentages are honest estimates.

**Last updated:** May 2026

---

## Vision

Build **Enterprise Operational Intelligence** for Saudi and GCC organizations:

```text
Governed orchestration from first request to live operations
```

Not ERP-only. Not cyber-only. One platform where Discovery, Blueprint, CEM, CyberCrow, and SAREA compose a coherent operating model.

## Explicit non-goals (public)

To avoid **architecture inflation**:

- Not cloning every Odoo module before second customer proof
- Not “AI everywhere” — AI remains quoted **extra services** in Blueprint
- Not open-sourcing provisioning internals or customer seeds
- Not production cloud until local pipeline sign-off complete

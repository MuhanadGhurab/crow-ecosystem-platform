# Arabic Alias Strategy (CROW.UAT.1)

Arabic friendly names on every primary field. Additional `aliasesAr` for commercial Saudi phrasing (مقاول، مطعم، متجر إلكتروني، عمرة، etc.).

Search normalizes and tokenizes Arabic script alongside English. Tests verify Arabic queries in catalog and relevance suites.

Do not transliterate English-only marketing terms without Arabic business equivalents where common.

## 2. Personal data we may process

Depending on your use of Crow, we may process:

### Identity and account data

* name;
* email address;
* authentication provider;
* account and platform identifiers;
* account state and verification evidence;
* role and membership evidence.

### Organization and request data

* organization name;
* industry;
* size and operational stage;
* organization contacts;
* selected services and modules;
* discovery responses;
* proposal, Blueprint, and onboarding activity.

### Legal and consent evidence

* accepted document type;
* document version and integrity hash;
* timestamp;
* locale;
* acceptance source;
* optional marketing preference;
* withdrawal or reacceptance history.

### Security and technical data

* authentication and session events;
* device and browser summaries;
* truncated or minimized network information;
* audit activity;
* access decisions;
* security-event and incident evidence;
* rate-limit and fraud-prevention signals.

### Support and communication data

* support requests;
* correspondence;
* incident reports;
* notification delivery records.

Crow does not ask users to place passwords, private keys, authentication codes, or production secrets in general-purpose forms.

## 3. Why we process personal data

Crow may process personal data to:

* create and secure platform accounts;
* authenticate and verify users;
* record legal acceptance;
* receive and review organization requests;
* conduct guided discovery;
* produce proposals and Enterprise Blueprints;
* manage onboarding and tenant readiness;
* provide authorized portal access;
* prevent fraud and unauthorized activity;
* monitor security and platform reliability;
* investigate and respond to incidents;
* comply with legal and regulatory obligations;
* communicate service and security information;
* send optional marketing only where lawfully permitted.

Processing may be based on contract, legal obligation, consent, legitimate interests where permitted, or another lawful basis available under applicable law.

## 4. Data minimization and purpose limitation

Crow seeks to collect only information reasonably necessary for stated platform, security, legal, operational, or contractual purposes.

Access is limited according to role, account state, organization relationship, and operational need.

Personal data must not be reused for an incompatible purpose without an appropriate lawful basis.

## 5. Data sharing and service providers

Crow may share limited personal data with approved service providers supporting:

* identity and authentication;
* cloud hosting;
* email delivery;
* monitoring and security;
* database services;
* support and communications;
* professional and legal services.

Providers must be subject to appropriate contractual, confidentiality, security, and data-protection requirements.

Crow does not sell personal data.

## 6. International processing and data transfers

Crow will assess hosting, processing, access, and transfer arrangements according to applicable Saudi requirements and contractual obligations.

Where personal data is transferred or accessed outside the Kingdom, Crow will apply required legal safeguards, assessments, approvals, or contractual mechanisms.

Specific customer data-residency commitments may be defined in an enterprise agreement or Data Processing Addendum.

## 7. Retention

Crow retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose, legal obligations, security, dispute resolution, evidence, and contractual commitments.

Legal-acceptance and security-audit evidence may be retained for extended periods where necessary to establish compliance, investigate incidents, or resolve disputes.

Retention periods should be documented in Crow's internal retention schedule and applicable customer agreements.

## 8. Security measures

Crow uses layered technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data.

These may include:

* identity and access management;
* least privilege;
* authentication and session controls;
* encryption;
* secure development practices;
* audit logging;
* monitoring and detection;
* vulnerability management;
* incident response;
* backups and recovery;
* supplier security controls;
* personnel responsibilities and awareness.

No security program eliminates all risk.

## 9. Individual rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to:

* receive information about processing;
* access their personal data;
* request correction;
* request destruction where applicable;
* withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
* object or raise a complaint;
* request information about disclosures or transfers.

Crow may need to verify identity and authority before fulfilling a request.

Some information may be retained where required by law, security, legal claims, or contractual obligations.

## 10. Marketing preferences

Marketing communications require an appropriate lawful basis and, where required, explicit opt-in.

Withdrawing marketing consent does not deactivate the Crow platform account or affect required service and security communications.

## 11. Security incidents

Crow maintains processes to identify, assess, contain, investigate, document, and respond to personal-data and cybersecurity incidents.

Where legally required, Crow will notify the competent authority, affected customers, or individuals according to applicable obligations and contractual arrangements.

## 12. Children

Crow's enterprise and business services are not intended for children unless expressly authorized and supported by an appropriate lawful basis and guardian process.

## 13. Changes

Crow may update this Notice to reflect changes in law, regulation, technology, services, or processing practices.

Material changes may require acknowledgement or reacceptance.

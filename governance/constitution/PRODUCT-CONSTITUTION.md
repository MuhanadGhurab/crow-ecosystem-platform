## 2. Product definition

> GHURAVIA is an adaptive social technology-learning world where users create a digital Crow identity, navigate structured learning Horizons, complete practical Missions, produce Evidence of capability, collaborate within a positive community and transform their progress into educational and professional value.

This is the **single authoritative** product definition. Downstream documents may summarize and must link here.

---

## 3. Product Pillars

Exactly six Pillars:

1. Learning and Development
2. Verified Skills and Evidence
3. Identity and Progression
4. Positive Community and Collaboration
5. Professional Opportunity
6. Security, Privacy and Trust

Rules:

- Commercial capabilities are **enabling** capabilities.
- Monetization is **not** a seventh learning Pillar.
- Every Feature must map to at least one Pillar.
- A Feature that maps to **no** Pillar **cannot** enter active Scope.

## 4. Permanent Product Principles

| # | Principle | Meaning | Required behavior | Prohibited behavior |
|---|-----------|---------|-------------------|---------------------|
| 1 | User Choice | Users decide their path within eligibility | Present options; allow Nest skip bands as defined | Force Routes; silent auto-enrollment into paid capacity |

| 8 | Security and Privacy by Design | Trust is foundational | Least privilege; auditable decisions; minimization | Collecting unnecessary identity data “just in case” |
| 9 | Learning Before Entertainment | Live/community serve learning | Learning outcomes for Live Sky launch slice | Metaverse entertainment without learning value |
| 10 | Controlled Expansion | Growth is gated | Scope classifications; change impact | Silent Scope creep via tickets or UI |

## 5. Product Integrity Rules

```text
Visual Identity ≠ Knowledge
Knowledge ≠ Skill
Skill = Knowledge Proven Through Evidence
Completion ≠ Mastery
Subscription ≠ Rank
Payment ≠ Prestige
XP ≠ Mastery
Hidden UI ≠ Authorization
AI Recommendation ≠ Final Authority
```

Commercial and authority separations:

```text
Payment controls access and capacity.
Learning eligibility controls readiness.
Evidence controls Mastery.
Trust controls social authority.
Prestige controls distinction.
```

Money must never directly grant XP, Mastery, approved Evidence, exam results, leaderboard position, Trust, or Prestige.

## 6. Product Non-Goals

GHURAVIA will **not** become:

- A generic course catalogue.
- An unrestricted social network.
- A pay-to-win platform.
- A platform where payment grants competence.
- A popularity-only leaderboard.
- An AI-controlled final assessment authority.
- A static dashboard exposing every capability.
- A full 3D metaverse at initial launch.
- A cryptocurrency or NFT economy.
- An uncontrolled user-generated course marketplace at launch.

## 7. Audience and safety direction

- Product direction: approximately **age 15 and above**.
- Activation for ages **15–17** remains **CONDITIONAL** on Saudi legal validation.
- If the required model is not legally validated, initial commercial launch may temporarily operate as **18+**.
- Younger-user safety must not depend only on age declarations.
- Exact birth dates and personal identity details are private.
- Unrestricted private messaging is **excluded** from initial launch (see Scope Baseline).

Audience examples (non-exhaustive): digital beginners, students, technology graduates, IT and cybersecurity professionals, developers, AI/ML learners, data practitioners, cloud/DevOps, robotics/IoT, game developers, designers, project/product managers, GRC practitioners, researchers, career changers, and technical leaders.

---

## 8. Regional architecture principle

```text
One Core Platform — Multiple Regional Experiences
```

Permits: shared core domains; regional policy packs; regional content packs; localization; regional Feature Flags; future data or hosting separation where required.

Does **not** permit uncontrolled codebase forks per region.

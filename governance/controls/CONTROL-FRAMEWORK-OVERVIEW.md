## Purpose

Describe the control families GHURAVIA intends to map during later security and compliance Gates. This document does **not** assert certification or full compliance.

## Control families (direction)

| Family | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| Identity and access | Account assurance A0–A3; passkey-first; authorization separate from UI |
| Privacy | Data minimization; PDPL mapping pending validation |
| Auditability | State-changing decisions auditable |
| Content integrity | Evidence quality; assessment integrity |
| Commercial integrity | Anti-pay-to-win; entitlement consistency |
| Operational resilience | Backup, recovery, monitoring |
| Regional policy | Saudi and global policy packs |

## Mapping language

Controls are **mapped to** or **informed by** recognized practice. Certification claims are forbidden until independently validated.

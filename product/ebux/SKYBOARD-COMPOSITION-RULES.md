## Composition rules

- Continue Your Flight is the highest-priority module when an active Flight exists.
- Locked modules may show reason (entitlement vs readiness vs trust) without implying permission.
- Empty modules show first-action guidance, not dead ends.
- Degraded mode: Continue Flight + Flight Log remain available if Live/Rookery/RAVEN advisory fail.

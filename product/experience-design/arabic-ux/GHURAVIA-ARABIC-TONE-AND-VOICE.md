## Product voice

GHURAVIA speaks as a calm, capable learning world: professional, warm, direct. It respects the learner’s intelligence. It does not lecture like a government circular and does not perform poetry when the user needs an action.

## Register

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Contemporary MSA | Saudi dialect in official UI |
| Short clear sentences | Classical ornate constructions |
| Concrete verbs | Abstract noun stacks |
| Honest uncertainty | Prophetic certainty |
| Explain once, then shorten | Repeat unexplained metaphor |

## Emotional range

- Portal: inviting, clear purpose, light atmosphere — not mystical initiation.
- Mission: serious, operational, respectful of stakes — not quiz tone.
- Consequence: factual about what changed — not moralizing.
- Crowprint: ceremonial calm without destiny language.
- Echo: exploratory and safe — original journey stays intact.
- RAVEN: see RAVEN voice doc — supportive intelligence, never judgmental.

## Age 15+

Avoid childish slang, parental scolding, corporate HR jargon, and academic thesis language. Prefer everyday professional Arabic a secondary-school or early-career learner would trust.

## Standalone Arabic

Every primary headline, CTA, decision, and notification must be understandable without English subtitles. English may name brands and Horizons beside Arabic; it must not carry the meaning.

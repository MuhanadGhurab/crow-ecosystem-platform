## Visual thesis

Obsidian depth, cyan signal light, restrained gold accent — a night-world portal where skill journeys feel like flights through a living digital sky. Atmosphere leads; UI structure follows. Cinematic Crow-world presence without requiring finished Crow binaries on every surface.

## Spatial composition

| Anchor | Composition |
|--------|-------------|
| **World Portal** | Full-bleed atmospheric sky plane; brand GHURAVIA as hero; one promise line; five Horizon silhouettes as distant world regions (not cards); single CTA cluster “ادخل العالم / Claim your wings”; Live Sky as distant weather band (visibility only — no false runtime) |
| **Black Signal** | Immersive Mission stage: topology/environment occupies dominant plane; RAVEN briefing as edge presence; signal feed as vertical ribbon; decisions as consequential actions in the field — not a form wizard |
| **Crowprint Debrief** | Dark chamber after flight: Flight Log timeline rises; Crowprint emerges as private seal; Suggested Lineage as provisional wing-hint; Echo as side corridor — not a quiz scorecard |

## Navigation behavior

- Portal: minimal chrome; language toggle; sign-in secondary to claim-wings.
- Mission: no generic stepper; progress as scene/tension meter and autosave whisper.
- Debrief: sequential reveal (outcome → consequences → Crowprint → lineage → route) with user control to expand.

## Arabic typography direction

- Display: expressive Arabic-first (IBM Plex Sans Arabic or governed equivalent when loaded).
- RTL default; generous line-height; headings with calm weight contrast.
- Gold reserved for rare emphasis, not body text.

## English typography compatibility

- Same family stack with Latin metrics; LTR islands for IDs only in admin/diagnostic contexts (never learner chrome).
- English subtitle may sit under Arabic as secondary, smaller, lower contrast.

## Surface hierarchy

1. Atmospheric world plane  
2. Primary narrative / decision  
3. Signal / status ribbons  
4. Secondary guidance (RAVEN)  
5. Utility (save, language, escape)

## Color-role model

| Role | Intent |
|------|--------|
| Obsidian | Depth, night sky, focus |
| Cyan | Signal, live change, guidance |
| Restrained gold | Rare ceremony (Crowprint reveal, Horizon mark) |
| Mist teal | Soft secondary surfaces |
| Risk crimson (muted) | Risk band — never neon alarm spam |

## Crow / RAVEN presence

- Crow: silhouette / atmospheric companion — **not** Production art; placeholder geometry until authorized assets.
- RAVEN: calm advisory edge — voice, not mascot spam.

## Topology treatment

Topology is a living district map: nodes dim/brighten with world-state; edges pulse on consequence. Change must be readable without color alone (shape/density).

## Mission tension treatment

Compressed atmosphere; signal feed urgency; decisions feel heavy; autosave reduces fear of loss without cartoon “points.”

## Crowprint reveal treatment

Quiet gold edge; private language; explicit provisional boundary; earned after debrief reflection — not instant badge pop.

## Motion language

Slow atmospheric drift; cyan signal ticks; topology morph on choice; Crowprint seal draw-in. Max 2–3 intentional motions per view.

## Reduced-motion

Replace drift with static gradients; signal ticks become opacity steps; topology updates instantly with text summary.

## Mobile transformation

Portal stacks: brand → promise → Horizons strip → CTA. Mission: topology collapses to summary diagram above choices; feed becomes drawer. Debrief: single-column reveal.

## Accessibility implications

High contrast on decision buttons; non-color band encoding; focus rings cyan-on-obsidian; skip to decision region.

## Technical feasibility

High for atmosphere via CSS gradients/SVG; medium for topology animation; fonts must be properly loaded (preflight gap). Fits CSS variables + modules/styled-jsx without Tailwind.

## Risks

Atmosphere may overpower comprehension if copy hierarchy weakens; gold overuse → luxury SaaS; topology decoration without state coupling → fails Mission goal.

## What makes it uniquely GHURAVIA

Night Crow-world + Arabic-first ceremony + consequential Mission topology + provisional Crowprint — not courses, not SOC, not LMS cards.

# Direction C — Challenger

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Code** | C |
| **Name** | Challenger |

## Visual thesis

The interface *is* the world. Regions of the screen are places: portal sky, Mission district, debrief chamber. Avoid boxed cards, stat strips, and multi-widget dashboards. Same obsidian–cyan–gold laws; composition becomes architectural.

## Spatial composition

| Anchor | Composition |
|--------|-------------|
| **World Portal** | Edge-to-edge sky; Horizons as geographic constellations in the sky plane (labeled in Arabic); CTA as threshold doorway; no marketing card grid |
| **Black Signal** | First-person-ish workspace over a district model; decisions appear as field interventions; signal events arrive as environmental phenomena; RAVEN as distant advisory silhouette/voice |
| **Crowprint Debrief** | Walk from Mission field into a quiet chamber; Flight Log as path markers behind you; Crowprint as private seal in the chamber center; Echo as a branching corridor you may enter |

## Navigation behavior

- Wayfinding by place metaphor (“enter the district”, “return to sky”) rather than tabs.
- Mission has no stepper; scene identity is environmental lighting / topology zone.
- Debrief is a single continuous space with focal shifts.

## Arabic typography direction

- Large poetic display for place names; body text carefully constrained for reading comfort.
- Risk: poetic type must not reduce Mission clarity — body remains high-contrast sans.

## English typography compatibility

- English place labels secondary; keep bilingual without duplicating entire spatial copy blocks.

## Surface hierarchy

Depth layers over boxes: far sky · mid topology · near interaction · utility ghosts at edges.

## Color-role model

Same palette; larger unbroken color fields; cyan as environmental signal weather; gold as chamber ceremony only.

## Crow / RAVEN presence

More environmental: Crow silhouette in sky; RAVEN as presence, not UI widget. Still non-Production art.

## Topology treatment

Primary hero of Mission. Must visibly reconfigure after decisions (paths close, services darken). Accessibility requires parallel textual state summary always on.

## Mission tension treatment

Environmental storytelling (alarms as sky color shifts, feed as audible-visual pulses with reduced-motion substitutes).

## Crowprint reveal treatment

Ceremony of arrival; seal forms; provisional boundary spoken clearly; avoid gacha/loot energy.

## Motion language

Spatial camera-like pans (subtle); topology morphs; chamber entrance. Strictly capped; easy to overdo.

## Reduced-motion

Disable pans; crossfade places; always show text state strip.

## Mobile transformation

Hardest: spatial metaphors compress to stacked places; topology becomes interactive mini-map; avoid horizontal-only critical actions.

## Accessibility implications

Highest risk of spatial UI harming keyboard users if focus order isn’t linear. Must keep explicit decision list and skip links.

## Technical feasibility

Medium–lower near-term: more custom illustration/SVG; harder responsive; still doable without Tailwind if SVG topology is designed simply.

## Risks

Looks “gamey” if over-animated; may confuse learners; implementation cost; accessibility debt if not disciplined.

## What makes it uniquely GHURAVIA

World-as-place — Living Missions as districts in a skill sky — maximum distance from LMS/SaaS while keeping identity laws.

# H1.1 — Accessible Motion Contract

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Status** | FOUNDER-REQUESTED REVISION |
| **Conflict rule** | Comprehension > atmosphere |

## Required

- Full content without animation  
- `prefers-reduced-motion` + manual toggle  
- No flashing · no motion-only meaning  
- Live regions for signals/consequences  
- Focus not moved unexpectedly  
- Drawer/overlay focus trap · Esc closes  
- Notifications accessible after dismiss (persistent log)  
- Crow ambient `aria-hidden`  
- Video: muted autoplay only · captions planned · pause control  
- Skip control on ceremonial sequences  

## Reduced-motion substitutions

Ambient → static gradients · topology morph → instant class swap + text · Crowprint → staged static panels · scroll depth → opacity 1 immediately · toasts → instant appear without bounce.

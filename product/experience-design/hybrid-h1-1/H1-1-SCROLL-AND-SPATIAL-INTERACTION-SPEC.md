# H1.1 — Scroll and Spatial Interaction Spec

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Status** | FOUNDER-REQUESTED REVISION |

## Allowed

Section reveal · depth transition · Horizon approach · timeline progression · topology focus · Crowprint assembly · Flight Log path reveal.

## Prohibited

Scroll hijacking · mandatory horizontal scroll · long pinned sections without progress · text moving while read · excessive parallax · animation delaying access · content hidden until animation completes.

## Portal scroll beats

1. Entry — atmosphere initializes; content readable immediately.  
2. Scroll — Horizons clarify and subtly approach spatial focus.  
3. Story sections — Living Missions / Crowprint / Evidence reveal without card grids.  
4. Threshold CTA — remains sticky/visible; ceremony optional.

## Access rule

Keyboard and reduced-motion users receive **full content** without scroll animation. Use IntersectionObserver for progressive enhancement only.

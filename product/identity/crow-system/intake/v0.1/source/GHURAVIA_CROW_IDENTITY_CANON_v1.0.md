# GHURAVIA Crow Identity Canon v1.0

**Status:** Canonical baseline — locked for project architecture  
**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Scope:** Base Crow species, 25 Lineages, visual differentiation, evolution, animation, hybrid safety, and rendering rules

## 1. Canonical lock

The following decisions are now project canon:

1. GHURAVIA has five Horizons: Operate, Build, Analyze, Protect, and Lead.
2. Each Horizon contains five Base Lineages, producing 25 Base Lineage models.
3. A Lineage is not a job, course, rank, purchasable skin, or static character model. It is a stable visual and behavioral expression of how a user repeatedly practices a capability.
4. Rank is only one coordinate of identity. XP alone cannot select or transform a Lineage.
5. A Cross-Horizon Major is a separate earned layer formed from Route competencies, an integration competency, a capstone, and verified Evidence.
6. Every Major has a unique Fusion Signature. Two Majors using the same Horizon pair must remain visually distinct.
7. The production Crow is deterministic. The same verified identity state must render the same Crow.
8. Payment may unlock access to learning opportunities, but it cannot grant Lineage, mastery, Evidence, Major identity, Prestige, or visual proof.
9. The full identity is rendered through a Living Profile World, an Adaptive Crow Portrait/Token, and a Compact Crow Mark.

> **Rank tells where a person stands. The Crow tells how they developed. The environment tells what they built, influenced, and lived through.**

## 2. The non-negotiable design principle

The 25 Crows must not be distinguished by recoloring the same model.

Every Lineage must be identifiable through at least four independent channels:

- Silhouette and center of gravity
- Feather topology and material treatment
- Posture and behavioral presence
- A unique motion verb
- An anatomical signature module
- A habitat landmark and environmental response
- A compact token glyph for small sizes

Color supports meaning, but it never carries identity by itself.

## 3. Locked GHURAVIA species invariants

All Lineages remain members of one recognizable GHURAVIA cyber-crow species. The following do not change arbitrarily:

- Core beak construction and facial proportions
- Leg and foot anatomy
- Obsidian-black feather base and realistic feather layering
- Overall crow skeletal logic and flight plausibility
- Core eye placement and expression range
- Material quality: advanced, integrated cybernetics rather than costume armor
- Absence of fantasy crowns, human clothing, literal job uniforms, or decorative weapons
- Evidence marks must be earned and authenticated
- Prestige is shown through restraint, presence, behavior, and environmental response—not excessive ornament

Origin and harmless presentation preferences may influence atmosphere, surface weathering, viewing angle, and habitat style without falsifying capability.

## 4. Anatomical visual grammar

Each body region has a stable semantic purpose. This makes Base Lineages readable and gives hybrids predictable places to integrate.

| Body region | Primary semantic role |
|---|---|
| Eyes and head | Perception, attention, investigation, and current focus |
| Neck and outer shoulders | Boundary strength, resilience, authority, and protective readiness |
| Wing framework and primary feathers | Built capability, Route depth, implementation, and connection architecture |
| Chest and sternum | Core identity, active Major bridge, and integrated mastery |
| Spine and underwing channels | Operation, continuity, automation, health, and service flow |
| Tail and flight-vector feathers | Direction, navigation, coordination, and future intent |
| Feet, perch, and ground contact | Proven foundations, completed work, and verified practical Evidence |
| Surrounding space and flock response | Contribution, mentorship, influence, Trust, and Prestige |

No Lineage owns a region exclusively, but its strongest signature should appear in its natural semantic region.

## 6. Universal signal language

Signal colors have one meaning across the whole ecosystem. They are not Horizon skins.

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Cyan | Healthy active state, verified system flow, or normal learning activity |
| Cobalt/blue | Construction, structured activation, or defensive response when context makes the meaning clear |
| Amber | Incomplete work, attention, controlled degradation, or recovery in progress |
| Crimson | Authorized adversarial probe, detected fault, or controlled test—never evil, rank, or personality |
| Violet | Validated fusion, learned Red–Blue junction, or confirmed Cross-Horizon integration |
| Pearl-white | Navigation, coordination, assurance confirmation, or highly restrained Prestige response |

Rapid strobing is prohibited. Ambient loops use smooth, low-contrast changes. Milestone effects play once. Reduced-motion mode replaces travel, orbit, and ripple effects with static state changes or slow opacity transitions.

## 8. Stable evolution rule

Each Lineage keeps three permanent recognition anchors from its first appearance through Prestige:

1. Its silhouette cue
2. Its anatomical/material motif
3. Its motion verb

Evolution increases integration, reach, proof, and restraint; it does not replace the identity with a new costume.

| Development state | Visual behavior |
|---|---|
| Flight-Ready | Universal clean base Crow after The Nest; no profession claim |
| Horizon Seed | One small material or signal feature appears in the Horizon's natural body region |
| Forming Lineage | Sustained behavior activates the Lineage's silhouette cue, first signature module, and compact glyph |
| Specialist | A major anatomical region carries mature Lineage topology; Route differences become visible |
| Integrated Raven | The technology becomes structurally seamless; Evidence and Cross-Wing elements connect coherently |
| Prestige Expression | Less ornamental movement, rarer signals, stronger presence, and greater environmental/flock response |

A Lineage change requires sustained contrary evidence and a transition period. A single Route, course, preference choice, or temporary activity cannot switch it.

## 9. Readability at three scales

| Output | Required readable information |
|---|---|
| Living Profile World | Lineage, Major, journey, portfolio, verified landmarks, relationships, ambition, and current activity |
| Crow Portrait/Token | Horizon family, Base Lineage, maturity, one active Major, and current state |
| Compact Crow Mark | Canonical Crow species, Lineage glyph, maturity band, and at most one activity indicator |

At 24–48 px, anatomy alone is insufficient. The Compact Crow Mark must use the Lineage's unique glyph and a Horizon-specific frame grammar. Exact facts such as XP, certificates, Evidence counts, Trust values, and dates remain structured profile data rather than being forced into the art.

## 10. Hybrid-safe styling rules

Cross-Horizon designs do not split the Crow into two differently colored halves.

Every visible hybrid uses these layers:

1. **Primary Lineage chassis:** controls center of gravity, dominant silhouette, and base posture.
2. **Secondary Lineage architecture:** occupies its natural semantic body region and modifies the primary form without erasing it.
3. **Major Fusion Signature:** a unique bridge mechanism at the sternum, wing root, or another semantically valid integration point.
4. **Mastery balance:** controls visual share and integration depth; typical ranges are 70/30, 60/40, and 50/50.
5. **One animation sentence:** communicates the integrated professional action, not two ambient loops playing simultaneously.
6. **Capstone landmark:** proves the integrated output in the environment.

The active Major owns the highest-priority Fusion Signature. Other earned Majors remain available in the profile record and environment but do not all appear as simultaneous hardware on the Crow.

Example distinction within Build × Operate:

- Site Reliability Engineering: precision lattice + operational heartbeat + error-budget orbit; **measure → risk rises → stabilize**.
- Platform Automation Engineering: framework scaffold + orchestration rail + self-service gateway; **request → provision → ready**.
- Distributed Connectivity Engineering: interface mesh + traffic channels + resilient bridge; **connect → route → reroute**.

The shared Horizon pair is visible, but the Fusion Signature, motion verb, and capstone landmark make each Major unique.

## 11. Deterministic identity model

The renderer should consume a versioned identity state rather than a free-form image prompt.

```text
CrowIdentityState =
  speciesVersion
  + developmentalState
  + primaryLineage
  + secondaryLineage?
  + activeMajor?
  + masteryBalance
  + routeDepth
  + verifiedEvidence
  + contribution
  + trust
  + prestigeClass
  + personalitySignals
  + originInfluence
  + currentActivity
  + accessibilityPreferences
```

The same normalized input and asset version must always produce the same anatomy, modules, token, and animation state. AI-generated concept art may discover the canon, but production identity is assembled from governed assets and rules.

## 12. Animation priority

Only one semantic animation sentence may dominate at a time.

1. Safety and reduced-motion preference
2. Current mission or risk state
3. Active Major Fusion Signature
4. Base Lineage ambient motion
5. Habitat atmosphere

This prevents hybrid users and advanced users from becoming visually noisy.

## 13. Visual acceptance gates

A Lineage design is accepted only if it passes all of the following:

- Recognizable in black silhouette without color or labels
- Distinguishable from the other four Lineages in its Horizon
- Distinguishable from its named collision risks in other Horizons
- Readable in a 512 px portrait
- Recoverable through its glyph at 24–48 px
- Still recognizable at Flight-Ready/Forming and at Prestige
- Compatible with at least four secondary-Horizon integrations
- Motion remains understandable with glow disabled
- Reduced-motion representation conveys the same state
- No decorative feature falsely implies Evidence, authority, Trust, or Prestige
- No purchasable presentation option can imitate an earned signature

## 14. Production asset strategy

The 25 Lineages do not require 25 unrelated anatomical rigs.

Use:

- One canonical skeletal rig and anatomy system
- Five Horizon material/geometry packs
- Twenty-five Lineage silhouette and module packs
- Versioned Route and Evidence overlays
- Unique Major Fusion Signature modules
- One semantic animation state machine
- Habitat landmark and environment packs
- Portrait and compact-token renderers derived from the same identity state

## 15. Items intentionally left open

These are implementation decisions, not identity ambiguities:

- Exact color values and material shaders
- Final front/side/three-quarter reference sheets
- Exact animation durations and easing curves
- Final Arabic localization of all Lineage and Major names
- Route syllabi and mastery thresholds
- Final asset technology choice between 2.5D, real-time 3D, or a hybrid presentation stack

They may be refined without changing the locked Lineage meanings or visual grammar.

This hierarchy is the binding rule for every future GHURAVIA Crow design.

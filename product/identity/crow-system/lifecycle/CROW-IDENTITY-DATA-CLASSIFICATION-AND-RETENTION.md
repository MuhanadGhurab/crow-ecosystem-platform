## Retention and rights principles

- Separate user-visible history from privileged audit history.
- Support export of understandable identity records.
- Support correction.
- Support deletion/pseudonymization subject to legal and integrity retention.
- Never expose private suggestion history in public export links.
- Preserve minimal historical references needed for deprecation/supersession and audit.
- Record unresolved legal retention requirements honestly.

## Definition

```text
A Mother Form is a shared Horizon-level visual structure
from which five Lineage variants are derived.

It is not a Lineage award.
It is not a Prestige class.
It is not a species.
```

Mother Forms must be distinguishable in full color, monochrome, thumbnail, without labels, without motion, and with reduced detail. Color may reinforce—never create—distinction.

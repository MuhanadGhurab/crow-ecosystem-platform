## 5. Preservation doctrine

Apply this portfolio change budget:

> **70% preserve · 20% refine · 10% challenge**

Preserve:

- believable GHURAVIA corvid anatomy;
- obsidian-black organic feathers with technology integrated into feather
  structure;
- premium realistic/cinematic treatment;
- near-black OLED-oriented value design;
- controlled silver-blue separation and restrained cyan signal accents;
- natural weight, talons, wings, tail, and body balance;
- five-layer identity hierarchy;
- 80% Horizon DNA / 20% Lineage specialization;
- one Strength Signature per future Lineage;
- separate mark namespaces and invisible Trust.

Refine:

- Mother Form silhouette clarity;
- camera and scale consistency;
- feather topology;
- head, wing, tail, and center-of-gravity relationships;
- 48 px recognition;
- neutral-idle and signature-action plausibility;
- rig and 3D continuity;
- material consistency across all five Horizons.

Challenge only:

- one structural interpretation per C option;
- within the same species, material world, identity laws, and Horizon meaning.

A Challenger is not permission to change genre, introduce costume, add armor,
increase neon, or produce a generic AI cyber-raven.

## 7. Candidate production sequence

### Stage 1 — Structural silhouettes

Create 15 full-body, solid-black Mother Form candidates:

- five Horizons;
- A, B, and C for each;
- one consistent three-quarter camera family;
- equal body scale and foot baseline;
- anatomy fully inside frame;
- no color, background, labels, marks, props, glow, or scenery.

Analyze A is the existing locked Lens Wing v2 silhouette. Remount it as the
incumbent; do not regenerate it merely for novelty.

Before proceeding, reject any candidate that fails a hard gate in
`FOUNDER-SCORING-SHEET.md`. Allow no more than two targeted correction attempts
per failed candidate. A correction may repair the stated defect only; it may not
quietly replace the whole concept.

### Stage 2 — Blind structural review

For each Horizon:

1. Shuffle A/B/C into blind labels `X`, `Y`, and `Z`.
2. Create one same-scale three-up silhouette board.
3. Create one 48 px test strip.
4. Create one five-Horizon blind board for every direction family.
5. Store the reveal map separately.

Do not score polished lighting before the silhouette passes.

### Stage 3 — Cinematic presentation

For every structurally eligible candidate, create:

- one generated source master;
- one exact `3840 × 2160` presentation PNG;
- one portrait crop derived from the same master;
- one thumbnail derived from the same master;
- one candidate traceability record.

Use the existing
`references/locked/CINEMATIC-PRESENTATION-STANDARD.md`.

Within each Horizon, A/B/C must share:

- camera family;
- subject scale;
- crop;
- value range;
- light direction;
- background depth budget;
- accent-color budget;
- detail budget.

The background may present the Horizon but may not explain it. The posture and
silhouette remain the semantic authority.

Do not claim native 4K or HDR unless the recorded pipeline truly produced it.
An upscaled 4K sRGB PNG must be labeled as an upscale and must not be called
HDR10, PQ, HLG, or Dolby Vision.

### Stage 4 — Fixed scoring and recommendation

Run the hard gates and weighted score exactly as written. Evaluate:

- GHURAVIA identity continuity;
- Horizon readability without color;
- ability to grow five Lineages;
- full-body, portrait, thumbnail, and silhouette performance;
- animation and 3D feasibility;
- resistance to generic AI cyber-bird drift.

Scores are advisory. Recommend at most one candidate per Horizon, state the
tradeoff, and do not select it.

### Stage 5 — Founder handoff

Produce:

- five blind A/B/C boards;
- one 15-candidate overview;
- 48 px structural tests;
- revealed labeled boards;
- completed candidate records;
- completed scoring sheet;
- recommendation and risks;
- founder decision register;
- gate report.

Finish with:

> `FOUNDER_DECISION_PENDING`

Do not promote candidates into a selected or production folder until the
founder explicitly records a choice.

# Mother Form A / B / C Contract

## Shared rule

All 15 candidates remain the same GHURAVIA species and obey every locked Mother
Form anchor. A/B/C vary the structural interpretation, not the product's genre,
status system, or visual universe.

| Direction | Change envelope | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Founder Anchor** | Minimal structural correction; maximum visual continuity | Preserve the chosen emotional identity |
| **B — Refined Anchor** | Improve silhouette, anatomy, topology, rig continuity, and production consistency | Make the anchor production-ready without losing its soul |
| **C — Challenger** | Change one major structural hypothesis only | Test whether a better solution exists under the same laws |

## Shared comparison controls

- Full-body three-quarter camera family.
- Equal body scale, ground line, and frame occupancy.
- One head, one beak, two attached wings, two legs, and two feet.
- Same species proportion boundaries.
- Same structural, lighting, material, and environment budgets.
- No Lineage Mark, Evidence Seal, Fusion Signature, Rank, Prestige, Wingprint,
  or Trust cue.
- No Cross-Wing integration.
- No child-Lineage personality as the Mother Form's identity.

## Operate — Grounded Flow

**Binding read:** grounded, reliable, alert.

Protected anchors:

1. Low-to-mid center of gravity.
2. Planted serviceable base.
3. Continuous back-to-tail flow axis.
4. Wings close to useful range.

| Direction | Controlled brief |
|---|---|
| **A** | Reconstruct the accepted Grounded Flow direction in the founder style: practical body, stable feet, gently forward horizontal feather flow, compact useful wings, alert present-focused head. |
| **B** | Preserve A while clarifying maintainable feather lanes, clean weight transfer, neutral-idle rigging, and a more recognizable back-to-tail operational keel at 48 px. |
| **C** | Test one alternative grounded-flow solution using a measured working-step relationship and locally articulated underwing channels. It must not become O2 speed, Build assembly, Protect enclosure, or Lead elevation. |

Reject: generic upright raven, blue circuitry as identity, elevated command pose,
broad wing display, or defensive armor.

Protected anchors:

1. Active wing-root architecture.
2. Constructive outward or upward span.
3. Articulated edge rhythm.
4. Shared load between torso and wings.

| Direction | Controlled brief |
|---|---|
| **A** | Preserve the accepted partial shoulder-bracket idea with attached natural wings, visible load paths, purposeful construction, and restrained founder-style feather engineering. |
| **B** | Refine A into a cleaner rig-ready structure: fewer but clearer couplers, stronger torso-to-wing load continuity, better folded-idle plausibility, and no detached modules. |
| **C** | Test one folded-scaffold interpretation in which nested attached feather groups imply assembly without a dramatic spread. It must remain constructive, not defensive, ceremonial, or gadget-heavy. |

Reject: generic spread wings, literal tools, floating parts, weapon-like shapes,
P2 mass, or O2 routing wedge.

| Direction | Controlled brief |
|---|---|

military stance, perfect institutional symmetry, or decorative authority.

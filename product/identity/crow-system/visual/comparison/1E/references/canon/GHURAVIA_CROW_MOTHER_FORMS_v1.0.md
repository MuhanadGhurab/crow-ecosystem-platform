# GHURAVIA Crow Identity — Five-Layer Readability and Horizon Mother Forms v1.0

**Date:** 2026-07-22  
**Authority:** Founder-directed design lock  
**Scope:** Five Horizon mother forms and the inheritance grammar for all 25 Core Crow Lineages  
**Status:** Mother-form system `LOCKED`; exact production art, rig measurements, shaders, and animation timing remain `LOCK-READY`

---

## 1. Lock decision

Every GHURAVIA Crow must read as one coherent identity through five ordered layers:

1. **Horizon identity**
2. **Role identity / Core Crow Lineage**
3. **Personality and temperament**
4. **Earned maturity potential and expression**
5. **Cross-Wing compatibility and, when earned, active Major integration**

These are not five badges, five costumes, or five competing effects. They are an inheritance stack. Each layer answers a different question and may not impersonate another layer.

The word **Role** in this document means the protected Core Crow Lineage archetype inside a Horizon. It does not mean a job title, organizational authority, paid permission, or guaranteed profession.

### Binding reading hierarchy

> **Mother silhouette shows the Horizon. Child architecture shows the Core Crow Lineage. Gesture reveals temperament. Structural refinement reveals earned maturity. A unique Fusion Signature shows the active Cross-Wing Major. Evidence Seals authenticate proof. Trust remains invisible.**

This supersedes the earlier shorthand that assigned the full silhouette to Lineage and feather topology to Horizon. The new hierarchy gives the five Horizons recognizable parent anatomy while preserving 25 distinct child identities.

---

## 2. What a mother form is

A **Horizon mother form** is a governed anatomical envelope inherited by all five Lineages in that Horizon. It defines:

- the primary silhouette family;
- the center of gravity;
- the resting relationship between body, wings, head, and tail;
- the dominant feather-flow grammar;
- the baseline motion rhythm;
- the anatomical routes through which maturity and Cross-Wing integration may develop.

A mother form is **not**:

- one fixed pose;
- a sixth Lineage;
- a Rank or maturity state;
- a personality;
- a job costume;
- a subscription skin;
- a Cross-Wing Major;
- a claim of Evidence, Trust, Prestige, or authority.

Each child Crow may vary strongly in proportion, posture, head character, feather cut, material rhythm, and gesture, but it must retain at least **three of its mother form's four protected silhouette anchors** in every normal portrait.

---

## 3. Shared GHURAVIA species boundary

All five mother forms remain one believable GHURAVIA cyber-corvid species:

- one canonical crow skeletal logic and plausible two-wing/two-leg anatomy;
- bounded, believable variation in beak, head, body, leg, wing, and tail proportions;
- organic obsidian-black plumage with technology integrated into feather structure;
- natural weight, balance, perching, and flight logic;
- a restrained semantic signal language in which color supports meaning but never identifies the Horizon by itself;
- no crowns, uniforms, human clothing, costume armor, decorative weapons, or humanoid bodies;
- no floating machinery used as a substitute for anatomy;
- no ornament that falsely implies Evidence, Mastery, Prestige, authority, or payment status;
- no visible or inferable Trust signal anywhere on the Crow or habitat.

Mother-form distinction must survive with color, glow, labels, environment, and accessories removed.

## 4. The five locked Horizon mother forms

| Horizon | Locked visual feeling | Internal art-direction handle | Dominant read |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Operate** | Grounded, reliable, alert | **Grounded Flow** | Stable field-ready body carrying continuous operational rhythm |
| **Build** | Inventive, crafted, constructive | **Constructive Frame** | Articulated structure visibly able to assemble, connect, and carry load |
| **Analyze** | Observant, cerebral, pattern-sensitive | **Perceptual Lens** | Gaze-led form organized around layers, comparison, and signal interpretation |
| **Protect** | Defensive, disciplined, high-awareness | **Guarded Mantle** | Controlled boundary wrapped around a protected center |

The internal handles are production shorthand only. They are not new public taxonomy or user-facing names.

### 4.1 Operate mother form — Grounded Flow

**Canonical visual sentence:** A practical Crow that is already connected to the living system, balanced close to the ground, ready to notice change and keep work moving.

#### Protected silhouette anchors

1. **Low-to-mid center of gravity** rather than an elevated or ceremonial stance.
2. **Planted, serviceable base** with believable weight through both feet or one clearly stable working step.
3. **Continuous back-to-tail flow axis** that reads horizontally or gently forward.
4. **Wings close to useful range**—tucked, half-open, or locally articulated rather than broadly displayed.

#### Anatomy and feather grammar

- Stable torso with practical, field-ready proportions.
- Feather rows follow lanes, loops, paired bands, status rhythms, or service channels.
- Joints and seams look maintainable rather than ornamental.
- Repairs may be visible, but they communicate continuity and care—not defensive armor.
- The head stays alert to present conditions; it does not look visionary, ceremonial, or predatory.

#### Baseline motion rhythm

> **Monitor → adjust → stabilize**

Operate motion is compact, repeatable, and economical. Even an energetic Operate child returns quickly to a stable working state.

#### Allowed child variation

Operate Lineages may become compact, aerodynamic, weathered, rhythmically expressive, or warmly receptive. They may change tail length, shoulder breadth, wing articulation, head angle, and working stance. They must not lose the grounded operational axis.

#### Maturity potential

The form matures through continuity, redundancy, and economy:

- visible corrections become fewer and more precise;
- separate channels integrate into dependable flow;
- recovery seams become cleaner without erasing their history;
- movement becomes calmer under greater operational load.

Maturity is never represented by making the Crow larger, brighter, more armored, or more decorated.

#### Cross-Wing compatibility route

Operate's semantic integration path begins in the **spine and underwing channels** and terminates at the **wing roots or sternum bridge zone**. A secondary Horizon may connect to that flow without replacing the grounded chassis. Dormant connection paths do not glow or claim a Major.

#### Reject if it drifts into

- Lead's elevated directional stance;
- Protect's enclosing mantle;
- Build's broad structural display;
- a generic upright raven with blue circuitry;
- machinery that replaces practical body language.

### 4.2 Build mother form — Constructive Frame

**Canonical visual sentence:** A Crow whose body visibly understands structure—parts relate, loads travel, and every opening suggests something can be assembled with purpose.

#### Protected silhouette anchors

1. **Active wing-root architecture** visible in the body's outer shape.
2. **Constructive outward or upward span** rather than a purely tucked body.
3. **Articulated edge rhythm** created by modules, joints, trusses, or interlocking feather groups.
4. **Shared load between torso and wings**, making the silhouette feel built rather than merely posed.

#### Anatomy and feather grammar

- Shoulders and wing frameworks are the family's most expressive anatomical region.
- Feathers behave like connected components: trusses, plates, couplers, scaffolds, seams, and interlocks.
- Controlled asymmetry is allowed when it communicates iteration or modularity.
- Practical mass, elegant structure, and minimal precision are all valid Build personalities.
- Tool-like logic must remain feather-integrated; literal tools, tool belts, and weapon-like attachments are prohibited.

#### Baseline motion rhythm

> **Place → test → lock**

Build motion reveals relationships between parts. It never becomes random gadget activity or spectacle without a constructed result.

#### Allowed child variation

Build Lineages may be tall and architectural, compact and experimental, broad and load-bearing, wide and connective, or monolithic and exact. A child may expose or conceal its construction logic, but the body must still imply deliberate structure.

#### Maturity potential

The form matures from visible assembly toward coherent architecture:

- experimental pieces become governed modules;
- load paths become clearer;
- interfaces become cleaner and more reliable;
- advanced forms can become visually simpler because the construction has become seamless.

More parts do not mean more mastery.

#### Cross-Wing compatibility route

Build's integration path begins in the **wing framework and shoulder couplers** and resolves at the **wing root or sternum bridge zone**. Secondary architecture must become load-bearing within the whole form; detached devices and crude half-body hybrids are rejected.

#### Reject if it drifts into

- O2's forward traffic-routing wedge;
- P2's defensive mass;
- generic spread wings with no structural logic;
- detached floating modules;
- “more cybernetics equals more capable.”

### 4.3 Analyze mother form — Perceptual Lens

**Canonical visual sentence:** A Crow whose attention shapes the entire body; the viewer sees where it is looking, how it separates information, and how layers become understanding.

#### Protected silhouette anchors

1. **Head-and-gaze-led composition** in which perception pulls the body line.
2. **Deliberate head offset, tilt, or depth angle** rather than a generic forward stare.
3. **Layered upper-feather architecture** that reads as comparison, depth, mapping, provenance, or synthesis.
4. **Controlled negative space around the active perceptual region**, keeping the visual question legible.

#### Anatomy and feather grammar

- Eyes, head, neck line, and upper feathers establish the first internal read.
- Feathers use nested layers, repeated patterns, contour logic, micro-etching, trace paths, or source convergence.
- Wings may behave like comparison fans, folded archives, maps, ledgers, or synthesis surfaces.
- The body may be inward, expansive, formal, skeptical, or expressive, but attention must remain specific.
- One glowing eye or a floating HUD cannot carry Analyze identity by itself.

#### Baseline motion rhythm

> **Observe → isolate → resolve**

Analyze motion begins with attention, not output. It becomes more selective as certainty improves.

#### Allowed child variation

Analyze Lineages may be alert and comparative, deeply folded and introverted, broad and spatial, narrow and evidentiary, or athletic and communicative. Their social energy may differ dramatically while the gaze-and-layer parent grammar remains visible.

#### Maturity potential

The form matures through selectivity and clarity:

- noisy scans become targeted observation;
- many visible layers resolve into fewer meaningful relations;
- provenance becomes integrated rather than dangling decoration;
- motion pauses lengthen because the Crow needs less spectacle to communicate depth.

Maturity does not mean a larger optic, more holograms, or more data floating around the body.

#### Cross-Wing compatibility route

Analyze's integration path begins in the **eyes, head, and upper-feather trace layers**, then converges through a governed path toward the **sternum bridge zone**. A secondary Horizon must change what the analysis enables, not merely add a second color or device.

#### Reject if it drifts into

- P1's distributed defensive vigilance;
- P3's pursuit stance;
- Lead's directive elevation;
- a generic blue-eyed “smart raven”;
- screens or effects that hide the Crow's own attention.

### 4.4 Protect mother form — Guarded Mantle

**Canonical visual sentence:** A disciplined Crow that defines a boundary around something worth protecting and can open, close, contain, or release that boundary with control.

#### Protected silhouette anchors

1. **Broad or pronounced shoulder mantle** establishing defensive readiness.
2. **Clearly guarded core** at chest, neck, or inner-wing space.
3. **Inward, enclosing, or perimeter-aware wing rhythm** rather than an outward display.
4. **Anchored stance** capable of resisting pressure without looking aggressive for spectacle.

#### Anatomy and feather grammar

- Neck, shoulders, outer wings, and perimeter edges carry the Horizon language.
- Feathers use dense overlaps, controlled angularity, boundary arcs, sensor apertures, and containment rhythms.
- The body may be tall, immovable, lean, sheltering, or precisely testing.
- Defensive presence comes from control and awareness, not costume armor.
- Crimson remains a tiny authorized test/fault signal where semantically justified; it is never a Horizon skin or villain code.

#### Baseline motion rhythm

> **Detect → brace → secure**

The final beat may become admit, contain, stabilize, validate, or release according to the child Lineage. Protect never attacks for spectacle.

#### Allowed child variation

Protect Lineages may stretch upward to watch, compress into a gate, lean into a trace, expand into shelter, or articulate a controlled test vane. They must retain a boundary relationship between the Crow, the protected center, and the outside condition.

#### Maturity potential

The form matures through precision and proportional response:

- boundaries activate only where needed;
- bulky overlaps become integrated control surfaces;
- defensive motion becomes calmer and more selective;
- recovery and safe release become as legible as containment.

Larger armor, harsher angles, and greater intimidation do not indicate maturity.

#### Cross-Wing compatibility route

Protect's integration path begins in the **mantle, shoulders, and outer-wing boundary seams** and resolves at the **wing root or sternum bridge zone**. A secondary Horizon must operate through or strengthen the boundary logic without turning one half of the Crow into armor.

#### Reject if it drifts into

- costume armor or a mechanical exosuit;
- decorative weapons or attack posture;
- villainous red identity;
- “largest Crow equals strongest protector”;
- Build's load-bearing mass without a protected-center relationship.

### 4.5 Lead mother form — Directional Presence

**Canonical visual sentence:** A composed Crow whose posture creates clarity, space, and direction; others could align around it without the Crow needing a crown or display of dominance.

#### Protected silhouette anchors

1. **Upright spinal and head line** creating elevation without arrogance.
2. **Clean, open shoulder relationship** that allows direction or formation to read.
3. **Commanding balance or intentionally controlled asymmetry**, never accidental imbalance.
4. **Directional tail, stance, or negative-space vector** that points beyond the Crow toward coordinated action or future intent.

#### Anatomy and feather grammar

- Head carriage is clean and composed; it never uses a literal crown.
- Long directional vanes, restrained formation lines, balanced mantle rhythms, and purposeful negative space carry the family.
- The body may create room, commit to a mission, listen laterally, hold formal assurance, or face an uncertain Horizon.
- Authority must come from behavior and composition, not clothing, rank insignia, thrones, or physical size.
- Environmental response may express contribution or separately governed Prestige, but never Trust.

#### Baseline motion rhythm

> **Survey → signal → align**

Lead motion is deliberate. The result should be visible in improved direction, formation, or coordination rather than a dramatic solo performance.

#### Allowed child variation

Lead Lineages may be welcoming, decisive, low-profile, formal, or exploratory. They may range from broad to slim and from symmetrical to branching, provided the form still creates a clear directional relationship beyond itself.

#### Maturity potential

The form matures through restraint and consequence:

- gestures become fewer and clearer;
- the Crow's stillness carries more meaning;
- alignment appears increasingly through the surrounding formation or path;
- visual authority becomes quieter, not more ornamental.

Prestige remains separate and can never be inferred automatically from upright posture or leadership capability.

#### Cross-Wing compatibility route

Lead's integration path begins in the **tail vectors, stance, and formation geometry**, then converges through the **sternum and surrounding directional field**. A secondary Horizon must be coordinated into one professional action; it cannot appear as a second costume or an orbit that falsely claims authority.

#### Reject if it drifts into

- crowns, uniforms, medals, thrones, or military costume;
- physical size as a shortcut for authority;
- excessive orbitals or followers used as status decoration;
- O4's technical sequencing without human/system direction;
- L5-like elevation applied indiscriminately to every Lead child.

## 5. Five-layer inheritance grammar

| Layer | Question answered | Primary visual carriers | What it may never claim |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Horizon identity** | “Which capability world shaped this Crow?” | Mother silhouette, center of gravity, wing-body relationship, family feather flow | Specific Lineage, job, Rank, Mastery, Evidence, Major, Trust, Prestige |
| **2. Role identity / Core Crow Lineage** | “How does this Crow contribute inside that Horizon?” | Child silhouette break, one dominant anatomical mechanism, motion verb, Lineage Mark | Profession guarantee, authority, proof, payment entitlement |
| **3. Personality / temperament** | “How does this individual behave?” | Head/beak character, expression, body proportion, feather cut, wear, material rhythm, signature gesture | Capability, morality, Trust, Rank, or skill level |
| **4. Earned maturity** | “How deeply and coherently has the capability become integrated?” | Structural refinement, economy of motion, controlled reach, Evidence-backed integration | Prestige, popularity, authority, payment status |
| **5. Cross-Wing** | “What approved integrated capability is active?” | Secondary architecture, mastery balance, unique Fusion Signature, one integrated motion sentence | Automatic Major from two colors, two courses, access, or self-declaration |

### Visual reading distance

| Viewing condition | Required read |
|---|---|
| **One-second / black silhouette** | Horizon mother form |
| **Small portrait / grayscale** | Core Crow Lineage within the correct Horizon |
| **Full portrait / short idle** | Personality and temperament |
| **Progression comparison** | Same Crow preserved while integration becomes more coherent |
| **Cross-Wing portrait or Living World** | Primary mother form remains dominant; secondary architecture and one earned Fusion Signature integrate semantically |
| **24–48 px compact mark** | Horizon frame + Lineage Mark; Evidence uses a separate authenticated Evidence Seal namespace |

## 6. Maturity-potential rule

Every final Lineage design must contain **growth capacity without false achievement**.

The artist or rig must reserve:

1. a Horizon-native refinement path;
2. a Lineage mechanism that can deepen without changing identity;
3. a neutral sternum/wing-root integration zone that can later host governed Cross-Wing architecture.

At an unearned state, these are structural possibilities—not empty badge sockets, inactive neon ports, or visible promises of future rank. When maturity is earned, the design evolves through coherence, efficiency, stability, and integration. It does not simply accumulate parts.

Prestige Classes—Elite Raven, Prime Raven, and Obsidian Raven—remain a separate system. Mother forms and maturity potential do not grant or imply Prestige.

---

## 7. Cross-Wing compatibility rule

Every mother form must accept any one of the other four Horizons while remaining recognizable as the primary chassis.

| Primary Horizon | Native integration origin | Governed bridge destination |
|---|---|---|
| Operate | Spine and underwing flow channels | Wing root / sternum |
| Build | Wing framework and shoulder couplers | Wing root / sternum |
| Analyze | Head, optic, and upper-feather trace layers | Sternum |
| Protect | Mantle, shoulder, and outer-wing boundary seams | Wing root / sternum |
| Lead | Tail, stance, and formation vectors | Sternum / directional field |

Compatibility is not qualification. No Cross-Wing expression becomes active until the approved Route clusters, bridge competency, capstone, verified Evidence, and Major governance are satisfied.

When active:

- the primary mother form retains the dominant silhouette and center of gravity;
- the secondary Horizon enters through its native semantic region;
- one unique Fusion Signature bridges the two;
- one integrated motion sentence takes priority;
- the result must not use a left-wing/right-wing recolor or two unrelated mechanisms placed side by side.

## 8. Relationship to the v0.2 portraits

The v0.2 Personality Signatures remain useful and are not discarded. Their strongest traits—temperament, body archetype, head/beak character, feather cut, material rhythm, and signature gesture—become **Layer 3 child traits**.

However, the v0.2 portraits do not yet prove the new mother-form system because:

- several Horizons do not share a sufficiently obvious parent silhouette;
- some cross-Horizon portraits remain closer to each other than to members of their own Horizon;
- pose sometimes carries more identity than inherited anatomy;
- maturity and Cross-Wing growth paths were not designed into every portrait consistently.

Therefore, v0.2 remains a personality concept library. The next image round must be a new version and must not overwrite it.

## 9. Acceptance gates for the next image round

### Mother-form gate

- All five Horizons are distinguishable as pure black silhouettes.
- A viewer can group the 25 Crows into five families without color, labels, glow, or environment.
- Each child retains at least three of four protected mother-form anchors.

### Role gate

- The five Lineages inside one Horizon remain distinct in grayscale.
- Each Lineage owns one dominant anatomical mechanism and one signature gesture.
- No child is distinguished only by size, pose, color, or quantity of machinery.

### Personality gate

- Temperament remains readable with all semantic light disabled.
- The same body archetype, head character, feather cut, and gesture are not reused as the dominant combination for another Crow.
- Personality never implies morality, Trust, capability depth, or authority.

### Maturity gate

- The Crow remains the same identity across early and advanced studies.
- Advancement looks more integrated and controlled, not merely larger or busier.
- No unearned study displays an Evidence Seal, Prestige expression, or active Fusion Signature.

### Cross-Wing gate

- Each mother form passes four secondary-Horizon studies.
- The primary Horizon remains identifiable in silhouette.
- Secondary architecture enters through a semantic body region.
- The Fusion Signature is unique to the Major, not merely to the Horizon pair.
- No split recolor, competing animations, or duplicated body mechanisms appear.

### Governance and accessibility gate

- Trust is absent and cannot be inferred.
- Payment, entitlement, XP, Rank, leaderboard position, and popularity are absent.
- Reduced-motion and glow-disabled states preserve the same meaning.
- Lineage Marks, Fusion Signatures, and Evidence Seals remain separate namespaces.

## 10. Production sequence now locked

1. Lock one dominant Strength Signature and one internal role energy for each of the 25 children.
2. Create one neutral monochrome reference sheet for each mother form.
3. Pass the five-way silhouette and same-species tests.
4. Rebuild the five child Lineages inside each approved mother envelope using the four-part identity framework.
5. Reapply and refine the v0.2 Personality Signatures.
6. Produce early/mature studies without Prestige or Cross-Wing claims.
7. Test each mother form with the other four Horizons using neutral, non-awarded compatibility studies.
8. Only after those tests, design approved Major-specific Fusion Signatures.

The one-axis and four-part individual differentiation system is specified in the companion **GHURAVIA Crow 25 Strength Signatures v1.0**. The next image artifact remains the **Five Mother Forms Monochrome Reference Board**, not another set of 25 fully rendered portraits.

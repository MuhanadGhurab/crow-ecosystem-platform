# Analyze Mother Form — Structural Direction Lock

**System:** GHURAVIA Crow Identity  
**Horizon:** Analyze  
**Mother Form title:** Perceptual Lens  
**Selected direction:** Lens Wing v2  
**Status:** Direction Lock  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Binding visual sentence

> Analyze is a grounded, composed Crow that deliberately compares a compact
> attached wing plane. Its intelligence is visible through the relationship
> between gaze, negative space, layered feathers, and stance.

The Mother Form is not a sixth character and does not imply a Lineage, Major,
rank, personality, maturity level, or Cross-Wing outcome. It is the visual DNA
inherited by all five Analyze Lineages.

## 2. Silhouette anchors

| Anchor | Binding rule |
|---|---|
| Camera | Full-body three-quarter side view |
| Center of gravity | Composed and slightly forward; neither crouched nor tall |
| Head | Directed diagonally across the near wing, not toward the ground |
| Beak | Clear and unobstructed; does not touch the wing |
| Near wing | Physically attached, shallow, compact analysis plane |
| Far wing | Folded and attached |
| Negative space | Narrow perceptual lens between beak, chest, and wing edge |
| Feet | Two feet grounded in a measured stance |
| Tail | Long, layered, directional counterbalance |
| Readability | Must remain recognizable as Analyze in solid black at 48 px |

## 3. Protected semantic read

The posture must read, in this order:

1. **Observation**
2. **Comparison**
3. **Pattern sensitivity**
4. **Quiet diagnostic focus**

It must not read as:

- Preening or grooming.
- Offering or presenting an object.
- Saluting, pointing, or commanding.
- Preparing to fly.
- Spreading wings for threat, warmth, or display.
- Holding a shield or forming a badge.
- Performing magic or controlling a hologram.

## 4. Horizon collision boundaries

| Neighbor | Analyze must not borrow |
|---|---|
| Operate | Low forward workflow posture or field-ready movement emphasis |
| Build | Broad construction spread, tool posture, or structural bracket gesture |
| Protect | Guarded enclosure, shield rhythm, or defensive mantle |
| Lead | Tall elevation, outward direction, or command gesture |

The near wing is not a Build workbench, Protect boundary, or Lead directive. It
is a feather-layer comparison plane whose meaning exists only because the head
actively relates to it.

## 5. Five-layer inheritance

| Layer | Analyze Mother Form authority |
|---|---|
| Horizon identity | **Defined here** through the Perceptual Lens silhouette |
| Role identity | Added later through one Analyze Strength Signature |
| Personality / temperament | Added through gesture amplitude and motion timing |
| Earned maturity | Added only through integration quality and restraint |
| Cross-Wing compatibility | Added only through a valid active Fusion Signature |

No later layer may erase the head-to-wing relationship or replace it with an
accessory.

## 6. Motion seed

The neutral Mother Form motion phrase is:

> **Stillness → head acquisition → shallow wing reveal → layered comparison →
> quiet reset.**

Animation constraints:

- The wing reveal is measured, never a flap or flourish.
- The head leads; the wing follows.
- The eye tracks across feather layers rather than a floating interface.
- The body remains grounded throughout the neutral loop.
- Motion must respect reduced-motion and flash-safety requirements.

## 7. Mark and entitlement boundaries

The Mother Form contains none of the following:

- Lineage Mark.
- Evidence Seal.
- Fusion Signature.
- Rank symbol.
- Prestige treatment.
- Wingprint.
- Public Trust cue.

The cinematic master contains small integrated signal traces only as neutral
material language. They are not glyphs, evidence, or entitlements.

## 9. Change control

Future iterations may refine feather contour, rig topology, proportions, and
micro-materials. A change reopens this lock if it:

- Removes the head-to-wing comparison relationship.
- Expands the wing into a dramatic spread.
- Creates a preening, command, shield, or flight read.
- Makes Analyze dependent on cyan, holograms, or environmental props.
- Introduces any protected mark namespace.

# GHURAVIA Mother Form Cinematic Presentation Standard

**Version:** 1.0  
**Established with:** Analyze / Perceptual Lens  
**Purpose:** Reusable finish standard for the five Horizon Mother Forms

## 1. Two mandatory masters

Every Mother Form must retain two separate assets:

1. **Structural master** — solid-black silhouette on a neutral field.
2. **Presentation master** — detailed 16:9 cinematic environment.

The presentation master may amplify emotion, material, and atmosphere. It may
not repair or replace a weak silhouette.

## 2. Canvas and framing

| Property | Standard |
|---|---|
| Presentation aspect | 16:9 landscape |
| Delivery dimensions | 3840 × 2160 PNG |
| Structural view | Full body; crown, beak, tail, legs, feet, and wing tips visible |
| Camera | Near eye level; restrained telephoto/compressed perspective |
| Subject height | Approximately 58–66% of frame |
| Negative space | Intentional and usable; never accidental empty darkness |
| Cropping | No anatomical crop in the Mother Form master |

The current selected generated source is 1672 × 941 and the supplied 4K file is
a high-quality Lanczos presentation upscale. It is exact 4K in pixel dimensions,
but it is not a native 4K model render.

## 3. OLED-oriented value design

“OLED” is treated as a visual design target, not a file format:

- Near-black background establishes depth.
- The Crow retains readable shadow detail; black feathers must not collapse
  into one shape in the cinematic master.
- Silver-blue midtone separation reveals anatomy.
- Peak accents occupy small areas: eye, beak edge, feather rim, and selected
  signal traces.
- Cyan is a restrained signal color, not a flood light.
- Large white panels, bloom, and full-frame neon are prohibited.

The PNG is standard 8-bit sRGB. A future HDR deliverable requires a separate
color-managed HDR pipeline and metadata; this package does not claim HDR10,
HLG, PQ, or Dolby Vision output.

## 4. Lighting hierarchy

1. **Structural key:** reveals head, breast, posture, and defining wing logic.
2. **Separation rim:** thin cool rim against the background.
3. **Material accent:** very limited highlight across the semantic feature.
4. **Environmental bounce:** faint, subordinate, and non-semantic.

Lighting may reveal the form. It may not become the form.

## 5. Environment grammar

The background uses three depth zones:

- **Foreground:** quiet ground/contact and restrained reflection.
- **Midground:** Horizon-specific architectural rhythm.
- **Far field:** atmospheric depth approaching black.

For Analyze, the midground uses concentric depth planes and smoked-glass
parallax. These are environmental echoes, not screens or interfaces.

Prohibited background shortcuts:

- Floating dashboards or readable UI.
- Dense hologram walls.
- Cyberpunk city color noise.
- Lightning, magical particles, or uncontrolled fog.
- Symbols that resemble Marks, Evidence Seals, Fusion Signatures, Rank, or
  Prestige.

## 6. Material hierarchy

| Priority | Material |
|---|---|
| 1 | Natural corvid feathers and believable anatomy |
| 2 | Graphite engineered under-structure |
| 3 | Sparse integrated signal traces |
| 4 | Environment reflection and atmosphere |

The Crow must remain a GHURAVIA cyber-corvid, not a robot wearing feathers and
not a natural bird covered with decorative circuit decals.

## 7. Identity boundary

A neutral Mother Form presentation may show:

- Horizon anatomy.
- Neutral cyber-organic material language.
- A Horizon-specific environment.

It may not show:

- A Lineage-specific Mark.
- Evidence.
- Rank or Prestige.
- Wingprint.
- Trust.
- Cross-Wing fusion.
- An earned maturity state.

## 8. Reuse rule

When the standard is applied to Operate, Build, Protect, and Lead, keep the
camera, canvas, black-level discipline, material restraint, and body scale
consistent. Change only:

- The Mother Form posture.
- The Horizon semantic lighting emphasis.
- The subordinate environmental rhythm.

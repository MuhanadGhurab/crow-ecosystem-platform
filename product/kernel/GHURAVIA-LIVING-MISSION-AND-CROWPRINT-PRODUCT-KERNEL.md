## Authoritative product statement

GHURAVIA is an Arabic-first living skill world where users enter
consequential digital Missions, make decisions that alter a persistent
world state, produce explainable Evidence, develop a private Crowprint,
receive advisory Crow Lineage suggestions, revisit pivotal choices
through Echo Flights and continue through a learning path shaped by
what they actually demonstrated.

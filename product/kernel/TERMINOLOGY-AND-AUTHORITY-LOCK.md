## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Living Mission** | A versioned, consequence-driven learning experience whose decisions alter deterministic server-authoritative world state. |
| **World State** | The bounded Mission state changed by validated learner actions. |
| **Evidence Signal** | Structured metadata emitted by a Mission decision or outcome. It is **not** automatically approved Evidence or Mastery. |
| **Crowprint** | A private, dynamic, explainable and provisional capability-pattern model derived from allowed Mission and Evidence metadata. |
| **Echo Flight** | A counterfactual replay fork from a prior Mission snapshot that allows the learner to explore a different decision without mutating the canonical completed Mission. |
| **Flight Log** | A learner-visible record of Mission actions, outcomes, Evidence signals, reflection and next-step recommendations. |

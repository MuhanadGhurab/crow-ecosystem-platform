## Purpose

Define **required fields** for Capstone blueprints that integrate prior Stage capabilities into an approvables Evidence bundle feeding Route-Proven (and CXW / SEX analogues) (§34).

```text
EXCEPTIONAL rubric level is not required for normal Route-Proven.
Expert review: NOT RUN. Pilot: NOT RUN.
No Product Code. No employment guarantees.
```

## Capstone design rules

1. Capstone **integrates** prior capabilities — it is not a disconnected quiz.
2. Capstone approval is required for Route-Proven qualitative eligibility.
3. Prefer synthetic sandboxes; never require real customer breach data.
4. PROTECT / security Capstones remain **defensive**.
5. CXW Capstones must show **integration delta**, not reuse of a single Route pack alone.
6. SEX Capstones remain **focused extension** of the host Route, not a new Horizon.
7. RT-ANL-001 Capstone remains **RESERVE / CONDITIONAL** until capacity activation.
8. Rubric **EXCEPTIONAL** is never a mandatory Proven gate.

## Depth limits (aligned with Mission §7)

Must describe problem, role, decisions, outputs, success recognition, and remediation path.

Must not include complete lesson prose, final answer keys, XP, Mastery percentages, or unsafe exploitation instructions.

## Explicit non-goals

* No accreditation.
* No “job ready” Capstone badge.
* No LOCKED Capstone content in 1C.

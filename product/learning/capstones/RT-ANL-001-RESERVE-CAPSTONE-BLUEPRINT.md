---

## Learner tasks

1. Frame / confirm the answerable question and ethics note.  
2. Profile quality; clean/prepare dataset; log seed ID and steps.  
3. Perform descriptive analysis with stated assumptions.  
4. Produce visualizations with alt text and uncertainty communication.  
5. Write decision-oriented report with limits and reproducibility steps.  
6. Disclose AI assist; assemble Capstone Evidence pack.

## Learner tasks

1. Clarify outcomes and stakeholder expectations under Capstone constraints.  
2. Define scope, decompose work, and produce delivery plan + RACI-lite.  
3. Map dependencies; prioritize with recorded rejected alternatives.  
4. Build risk register excerpt with residual risk.  
5. Write decision record, progress/escalation note as needed, and lessons.  
6. Assemble Capstone Evidence pack; pass title-language audit; disclose AI assist.

## Purpose

Define how GHURAVIA distinguishes **Team results** from **individual contribution** for Team and Live Sky Missions (§39).

```text
A learner must not receive full individual capability credit solely because the Team succeeded.
Final scoring remains pending GHV.PROGRESSION.1.
Expert review: NOT RUN. Pilot: NOT RUN.
```

---

## Contribution kinds

| Kind | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Team result** | Collective outcome (shipped demo, shared brief, facilitated exercise result) |
| **Individual contribution** | Person-specific actions and authored artifacts toward the Team result |
| **Leadership contribution** | Coordination, facilitation, decision ownership (when assigned) |
| **Documentation contribution** | Shared notes, run records, Evidence packaging |
| **Support contribution** | Enabling work (test help, peer coaching within rules) |
| **Observer contribution** | Limited / shadow participation — **does not** equal full practical credit |

---

## Binding rule — no free-riding Proven

* Team success is **necessary context**, not sufficient individual proof.
* Route-Proven / Capstone credit requires **individual contribution Evidence** meeting the Mission’s standard.
* Observer-only participation cannot claim full practical capability.

---

## Required contribution signals

May include (Mission Blueprint selects applicable set):

* Assigned role.
* Action record.
* Authored artifact.
* Decision explanation.
* Peer confirmation.
* Reviewer confirmation.
* Reflection.

Evidence class: **TEAM_CONTRIBUTION** (see [EVIDENCE-CLASSIFICATION.md](./EVIDENCE-CLASSIFICATION.md)).

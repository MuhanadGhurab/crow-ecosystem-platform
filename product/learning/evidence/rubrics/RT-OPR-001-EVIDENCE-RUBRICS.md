## Purpose

Provide **short qualitative rubrics** for RT-OPR-001 Evidence anchors **EVD-01…03** and Capstone **CAP-01**, using a **subset** of the Launch Evidence Value Matrix dimensions.

## Qualitative levels (shared)

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Incomplete** | Missing required artifact elements; unsafe (secrets); or not seed-bound |
| **Developing** | Present but unclear, weakly linked to lab, or incomplete redaction |
| **Meets** | Reviewable in ≤15–20 min; seed-bound; safe; demonstrates Stage outcomes |
| **Strong** | Clear, portable, residual risk noted; high authenticity trail |

Acceptance for Route progression: **Meets** or **Strong** on required dimensions (expert thresholds pending).

## Dimension subset (from Launch Evidence Value Matrix)

Used across OPR rubrics:

1. **Authenticity**  
2. **Relevance**  
3. **Reviewability**  
4. **Privacy** (incl. redaction)  
5. **Plagiarism / originality**  
6. **AI-assist integrity**  
7. **Professional value** (qualitative signal only — no employment claim)

*(Accessibility & Storage noted as Watch items; not scored in this short blueprint.)*

## RT-OPR-001-EVD-01 — Config Evidence

| Dimension | Incomplete | Developing | Meets | Strong |
|-----------|------------|------------|-------|--------|
| **Authenticity** | No lab seed / generic paste | Weak link to lab | Seed ID + before/after tied to learner lab | Seed + timestamped change trail |
| **Relevance** | Unrelated dump | Partial config | Sanitized snapshot matches STG-04 outcomes | Shows guardrail-aware change |
| **Reviewability** | Huge unfiltered export | Hard to navigate | Checklist-ready ≤15–20 min | Annotated diff + short note |
| **Privacy** | Secrets/PII present | Partial redaction | Redaction checklist complete | Extra scrub of identifiers |
| **Plagiarism** | Instructor key copy | Near-template only | Personalized to seed | Distinct narrative + config |
| **AI-assist** | Undeclared AI paste | Vague disclosure | Disclosure + learner verification | Explain-your-diff vs AI draft |
| **Professional value** | None | Thin | Usable ops hygiene sample | Portfolio-ready (lab-branded) |

**Accept if:** Meets+ on Authenticity, Privacy, Reviewability; no secrets.

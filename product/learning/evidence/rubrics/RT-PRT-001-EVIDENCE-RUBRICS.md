## RT-PRT-001-EVD-01 — Triage Evidence

| Dimension | Accept when… | Revise when… | Fail when… |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **Prioritization** | Alerts ranked with clear rationale tied to scenario impact | Ranking present but thin rationale | No prioritization or random order |
| **FP/TP reasoning** | Separates false-positive vs true-positive hypotheses with evidence cites | Labels without cites | Invents live activity or offensive checks |
| **Proportionality** | Response asks match severity and lab authority | Over/under-reaction without reflection | Recommends unauthorized real-world actions |
| **Artifact integrity** | Seed/variant cited; lab artifacts only | Missing seed citation | Claims production/live targets |

**Primary Missions:** RT-PRT-001-STG-04-MSN-01

---

## RT-PRT-001-EVD-02 — Timeline Evidence

| Dimension | Accept when… | Revise when… | Fail when… |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **Completeness** | Key events sequenced with timestamps and sources | Gaps noted but major events missing | Empty or unordered dump |
| **Defensiveness** | Timeline supports defensive investigation narrative | Speculative jumps without marking confidence | Offensive reconstitution of attacker tradecraft as how-to |
| **Gap honesty** | Unknowns labeled | Unknowns hidden | Fabricated certainty |
| **Integrity** | Unique seed; no live-target claims | Weak seed linkage | Real victim/org data |

**Primary Missions:** RT-PRT-001-STG-04-MSN-03 (supports STG-05)

---

## RT-PRT-001-EVD-03 — Investigation report

| Dimension | Accept when… | Revise when… | Fail when… |
|-----------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| **Report quality** | Clear findings, hypotheses, and asks; synthetic IOCs only | Structure OK but reasoning thin | Unsanitized dumps; live IOC claims |
| **Escalation brief** | Facts / hypotheses / asks separated; proportionate | Vague escalation | Fear-mongering or authority inflation |
| **Ethics** | Current ethics declaration included | Missing attestation refresh | Ethics gate not passed / unsafe content |
| **Evidence handling** | Chain notes / sanitization observed | Incomplete handling note | Tampering or real PII |

**Primary Missions:** RT-PRT-001-STG-05-MSN-02 · RT-PRT-001-STG-05-MSN-03

## Purpose

Define what is frozen after successful GHV.LEARNING.1D and which change classes apply.

```text
LOCKED means LOCKED AS GOVERNED DESIGN BASELINE
Revisions after Expert Review or Pilot require Change Request + new document version + register updates
No silent modification
```

---

## Frozen after 1D

* P0 Route IDs
* Route capability boundaries
* Stage IDs
* Mission Blueprint IDs
* Evidence Anchor IDs
* Capstone IDs
* Cross-Wing and Secure Extension boundaries
* Route-Proven qualitative rules
* Reserve status of RT-ANL-001 (LOCKED AS RESERVE DESIGN BASELINE — NOT A CONTROLLED-LAUNCH COMMITMENT — CAPACITY CONDITIONAL)

---

## Allowed without Material Change Request (editorial)

* Typographical corrections
* Clearer wording with unchanged meaning
* Reference updates
* Source-date updates
* Accessibility clarifications
* Arabic terminology corrections that do not alter capability

---

## Requires Controlled Change Request

* Adding or removing a Mission
* Changing a mandatory Stage
* Changing a prerequisite
* Changing Evidence requirements
* Changing Capstone scope
* Changing Cross-Wing source requirements
* Changing Secure Extension boundaries
* Promoting ANALYZE (RT-ANL-001) to launch
* Changing Route-Proven conditions

Controlled CR must: create a new document version; preserve prior baseline history; update Decision Register; update Baseline Manifest; update affected graph, Mission, and Evidence records.

---

## Requires Foundational Rebaseline

* Changing Horizons
* Changing the learning hierarchy
* Changing Evidence-before-Mastery
* Allowing payment to replace learning requirements

## Explicit non-claims

* Freeze does not mean Expert Approved, Pilot Validated, Publication Ready, Accredited, or Certified.
* No XP · No numeric Mastery · No Product Code.

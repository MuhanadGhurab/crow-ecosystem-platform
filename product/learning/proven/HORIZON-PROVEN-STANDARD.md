## Purpose

Define what **Horizon-Proven** means at architecture level, and lock the launch limitation that it is **not awardable** from the initial limited catalogue as a false claim.

## Foundation model

| Principle | Statement |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Breadth required** | Horizon-Proven requires **breadth across a governed portion of a Horizon**, not a single narrow artifact. |
| **One foundation Route insufficient** | **One launch foundation Route is not sufficient** to produce Horizon-Proven status (Graph Invariant 25). |
| **Limited catalogue** | Horizon-Proven will **not normally be awardable** during the initial limited catalogue. |
| **Progress without award** | The system **may display progress toward Horizon-Proven** without falsely awarding it. |
| **Future bundles** | Future required Route / Evidence bundles per Horizon remain **pending the expanded catalogue**. |
| **Wing Key** | Wing Key eligibility remains **future governed functionality** — not granted by 1B architecture alone. |

## Relationship to Route-Proven

```text
Route-Proven (qualitative, per Route)
        ↓ contributes signals / progress
Horizon-Proven (breadth across Horizon — AWARDING DEFERRED)
        ↓ later may inform
Wing Key / professional identity constructs (future)
```

Completing **RT-OPR-001**, **RT-BLD-001**, **RT-PRT-001**, **RT-LED-001**, or reserve **RT-ANL-001** alone must **never** auto-award Horizon-Proven.

Cross-Wing (**CXW-001**) and Secure Extension (**SEX-001**) may contribute to *progress displays* or future bundle definitions, but do **not** by themselves award Horizon-Proven in the launch catalogue.

## Allowed UX at launch

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| “Progress toward Horizon-Proven” indicators | Awarding Horizon-Proven badges/status from one Route |
| Explaining that more Routes will be required later | Implying OPERATE/BUILD/etc. completion = Horizon mastery |
| Linking to Route-Proven as a building block | Selling Horizon-Proven as a subscription perk |

## Awarding deferred — criteria for later Gates

Before awarding is enabled, later Gates must define:

1. Governed Route / Evidence bundle per Horizon.
2. Integrity / Trust conditions (PROGRESSION.1).
3. Catalogue lock including enough Routes for breadth (typically post-1D expansion).
4. Change Control for any early exception.

Until then:

```text

```

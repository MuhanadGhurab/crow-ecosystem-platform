## Purpose

Define **Route-Proven** as a qualitative achievement standard for launch Routes (and analogous standards for CXW / SEX where stated). This Gate does **not** invent progression formulas.

```text
STATUS: QUALITATIVE STANDARD DEFINED
No LOCKED Routes. No XP numbers. Expert review: NOT RUN.
```

## Eligibility conditions (qualitative)

A learner becomes **eligible for Route-Proven status** only when **all** of the following hold:

1. **All mandatory Stages** for that Route are completed.
2. **All mandatory assessments** meet their governed standard.
3. **Required Evidence** for that Route is **approved**.
4. The **Capstone** is **approved**.
5. **No unresolved integrity issue** exists on the Route Evidence pack.
6. **Required remediation** is complete.
7. **Applicable Trust conditions** are satisfied (thresholds PENDING GHV.PROGRESSION.1).

Until PROGRESSION.1, Trust conditions are recorded as a **placeholder gate** — they must not be invented as numeric scores in 1B docs.

## Binding clarifications

| Rule | Statement |
|------|-----------|
| Completion ≠ Proven | **Route completion is not automatically Route-Proven.** Completing Stages alone is insufficient without approved Evidence + Capstone + integrity clear. |
| Not a subscription benefit | **Route-Proven is not a subscription benefit.** Commercial entitlement must not grant or imply Proven status. |
| Cross-Wing unlock (later) | **Route-Proven may later unlock Cross-Wing eligibility** (e.g. source readiness for CXW-001), subject to PROGRESSION.1 and CXW architecture gates. |
| Evidence revocation | **Evidence revocation may trigger governed re-evaluation** of Route-Proven (and dependent Unlocks such as Extension / Cross-Wing eligibility). |
| Content updates | **Content updates do not automatically erase historical Route-Proven status.** Freshness / revalidation policies may require new Evidence for *new* claims, but past Proven is not silently wiped by catalogue edits. |

## What Route-Proven is not

- Not employment readiness or a professional title.
- Not Horizon-Proven (see [HORIZON-PROVEN-STANDARD.md](./HORIZON-PROVEN-STANDARD.md)).
- Not a Product Code or paid plan perk.
- Not awarded from Nest completion alone.
- Not awarded from quiz-only or passive completion records.

## Unlock linkage (architecture)

Typical Unlock type: **ULK-PRV-001 — Route-Proven review eligibility** (submit for review; does **not** auto-award Proven).

Downstream (later gates):

- May contribute to **Cross-Wing eligibility** when source Routes are Proven (or meet governed partial-entry policy).
- May contribute to **Secure Extension** entry policy when host Route is Proven (or partial-entry policy explicitly allows otherwise).

## Revocation sketch

| Trigger | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| Integrity failure on required Evidence | Approval revoked; Route-Proven suspended pending remediation |
| Capstone revoked | Route-Proven ineligible until Capstone re-approved |
| Policy / Trust change (PROGRESSION.1) | Governed re-evaluation path — no silent mass erase without Change Control |

## Explicit non-goals

- No XP, Prestige, or Mastery point formulas.
- No automatic Horizon-Proven from one Route.
- No LOCKED Route status in this document.

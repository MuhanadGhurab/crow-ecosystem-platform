## Purpose

Govern how GHURAVIA selects launch Routes from evidence — not trends, preference, or marketing.

## Locked learning hierarchy (unchanged)

```text
World → Horizon → Route → Stage → Mission → Evidence → Unlock
```

Horizons: OPERATE · BUILD · ANALYZE · PROTECT · LEAD · Nest (foundational layer)

## Source hierarchy

| Tier | Class | Use |
|------|-------|-----|
| 1 | Official role/skills frameworks | Primary capability language |
| 2 | Official technology documentation | Tool skills; not whole philosophy |
| 3 | Saudi strategic/regulatory sources | Relevance; **not endorsement** |
| 4 | Employment evidence (multiple ads/roles) | Recurring task patterns |
| 5 | Professional/academic evidence | Validation of methods |
| 6 | Community signals | Supplementary only |

Community popularity never overrides Tier 1–3 evidence.

## Freshness model

| Class | Examples | Review cadence |
|-------|----------|----------------|
| Stable Foundation | Networking, OS, SE fundamentals, risk | 18–24 months |
| Slow-Changing Practice | Cloud patterns, DevOps, IAM, SecOps, data eng | 12 months |
| Fast-Changing Technology | GenAI tools, threat TTPs, vendor UIs | 3–6 months |
| Vendor-Specific Detail | Console steps, product names | On major release |
| Regulatory/Standards | Frameworks, controls, blueprints | On edition change |

These are content-governance baselines, not legal commitments.

## Decision rules for recommendation

A Route may be **RECOMMENDED FOR LAUNCH** only if:

1. ≥3 meaningful supporting sources  
2. ≥1 authoritative (Tier 1–3) source  
3. Clear real-world tasks  
4. Feasible practical Evidence  
5. Expressible prerequisites  
6. Understood maintenance + lab burden  
7. Saudi + international relevance assessed  
8. Risks documented  
9. Scorecard complete (100-point model)

Cross-Wing: evidence from all source Horizons.  
Secure Extension: clear source capability being secured.

Final lock deferred to **GHV.LEARNING.1D**. Status in 1A: never `LOCKED`.

## Capability type taxonomy

FOUNDATIONAL · OPERATIONAL · BUILDING · ANALYTICAL · SECURITY · GOVERNANCE · COLLABORATIVE · INTEGRATIVE · SPECIALIST

## Portfolio shape target

```text
4–6 complete launch Routes
+ 1 Cross-Wing Route
+ 1 Secure Extension
(+ Nest separate)
```

Beyond that requires founder-capacity impact statement.

## Research limitations (mandatory acknowledgment)

- Public role descriptions may overstate requirements.  
- Job titles vary between organizations.  
- Vendor frameworks may promote vendor products.  
- Certification objectives ≠ workplace competence.  
- Saudi and international expectations may differ.  
- Rapidly changing tools invalidate details.  
- Professional judgment and expert review remain required.

## Purpose

Register public evidence used to shape launch learning capabilities. Entries record **URL, organization, version/date, confidence, tier, and limitations**. Content is summarized at research level only.

## Mandatory research posture

1. **Summarize only** — do not reproduce proprietary certification exam content or paid frameworks wholesale.  
2. **Strategic Saudi alignment is not endorsement** — Tier 3 sources inform national relevance; they do not imply GHURAVIA accreditation, regulatory approval, or official partnership.  
3. **No employment promises** — sources describe capability demand patterns; Routes never guarantee jobs, salaries, visas, or placement.  
4. **One Route ≠ one role** — Routes bundle learnable capabilities; roles below are research anchors only (see Role-Capability Matrix).

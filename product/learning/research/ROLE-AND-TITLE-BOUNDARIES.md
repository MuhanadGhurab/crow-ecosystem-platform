## Purpose

Draw hard boundaries between **Routes**, **roles** (research anchors), **Professional Titles**, **vendor certifications**, **Prestige**, and marketing language — so GHURAVIA does not imply employment readiness or inflate seniority.

```text
Final title issuance rules → GHV.PROGRESSION.1
This document is RESEARCH BASELINE only.
```

All launch portfolio IDs (RC-OPR-001, RC-BLD-001, RC-PRT-001, RC-LED-001, RC-ANL-001, CXW-001, SEX-001) remain **NOT LOCKED**.

## Core distinctions

| Construct | Is | Is not |
|-----------|----|--------|
| **Route** | Sequenced learning product (Stages → Missions → Evidence → Unlock) | A job, job offer, or employment-readiness certificate |
| **Role (matrix)** | Labour-market / framework research anchor for design | A title granted to the learner |
| **Professional Title** | Governed credential-like recognition requiring Mastery + Evidence bundle | Something earned by activity clicks alone |
| **Vendor certification** | External credential the learner may hold | GHURAVIA Product outcome or substitute for GHURAVIA Evidence |
| **Prestige** | Separate recognition economy (community/contribution signals) | Proof of job competence or senior rank |
| **Capstone / Live Sky** | Evidence-producing learning experiences | Automatic promotion to senior titles |

## Binding research rules

### 1. Route ≠ employment readiness

Completing a Route, Cross-Wing, Secure Extension, Nest band, or capstone:

- Demonstrates **learning Evidence** under GHURAVIA rules  
- Does **not** promise hiring, promotion, salary, visa, contractor award, or “job-ready” guarantees  
- Must not be marketed as employment placement  

Scenario “learner roles” inside Missions (e.g. junior operator) are **fiction for practice**, not conferred titles.

### 2. Professional Titles need a governed Mastery + Evidence bundle

A **Professional Title** (if/when issued under PROGRESSION.1) MUST require:

1. Declared **Mastery** thresholds on named capabilities  
2. Accepted **Evidence** artifacts (not quiz-only)  
3. Governance checks (integrity, and Trust where applicable)  
4. Explicit title policy version  

Activity metrics alone (time-on-platform, streak, video completion, attendance without Evidence) **cannot** mint Professional Titles.

### 3. Vendor certifications are external Evidence only

| Allowed | Forbidden |
|---------|-----------|
| Learner may submit vendor cert as **external Evidence** supporting a claim | Treating vendor cert as automatic GHURAVIA Professional Title |
| Reference certs in ROLE matrix as **REFERENCE ONLY** | Bundling or reselling certs as GHURAVIA Product Codes in this Gate |
| Map cert domains loosely to Horizons for design research | Claiming GHURAVIA is a partner / endorsed issuer without agreement |

Vendor cert ≠ Route unlock ≠ Professional Title.

### 4. No activity-only job titles

Forbidden learner-facing grants based solely on:

- Finishing videos or readings  
- Forum posts or Prestige events without Evidence  
- Single quiz scores  
- Purchasing access without Mastery/Evidence  

Allowed: descriptive progress labels (“Stage 3 in progress”) that are clearly **not** Professional Titles or job titles.

### 5. No misleading senior titles on beginner Routes

Beginner and foundation Routes (including launch set below) MUST NOT confer or display language such as:

- Senior · Staff · Principal · Architect (senior sense) · Head of · Director · Manager (employment sense) · Lead Engineer (senior sense)

LEAD Horizon foundation (RC-LED-001) teaches delivery/risk literacy; it does **not** mint “Engineering Manager” or “Head of Digital.” Capstone scenario role “delivery coordinator” stays scenario-scoped.

### 6. Prestige is separate

Prestige (community standing, contribution recognition, Live Sky participation badges, etc.):

- May coexist with learning progress  
- Must be visually and verbally **distinct** from Mastery and Professional Titles  
- Must not be sold as employment readiness  

### 7. Cross-Wing and Secure Extension naming

| Construct | Title risk control |
|-----------|-------------------|
| CXW-001 Secure Application Delivery | Name describes capability; not “Secure Architect” or job title |
| SEX-001 Secure Cloud Operations Extension | “Extension” language required; not a full PROTECT senior credential |

Reject title mashups without Atlas / Evidence (RISK-LRN-003 spirit).

## Purpose

Lock permanent architecture invariants for the GHURAVIA progression ecosystem. These rules bind all later formula work (1B+), Product Code, UX copy, and eligibility engines. They do not define numeric thresholds.

Constitutional alignment: Payment controls access and capacity; Evidence controls Mastery; Trust controls social authority; Prestige controls distinction.

---

## A. Gate §7 permanent separation rules

Exact total locked in this section: **16** payment / evidence / standing rules.

### A1. Payment does not create XP

Commercial payment, plan purchase, renewal, or upgrade must never write Flight XP. XP arises only from validated progression-eligible activity sources.

### A2. Payment does not create Momentum

Payment must never place a learner into a Momentum League or award season Momentum points.

### A3. Payment does not create Maturity

Payment must never advance Maturity Rank.

### A4. Payment does not create Mastery

Payment must never create Capability Mastery, Route Mastery, or Route-Proven standing.

### A5. Payment does not create Breadth

Payment must never create Horizon breadth or multi-path Breadth records.

### A6. Payment does not create Trust

Payment must never raise Trust Standing or clear integrity sanctions.

### A7. Payment does not grant a Professional Title

Titles are review outcomes over Evidence/Mastery/Trust eligibility — never sold.

### A8. Payment does not grant Prestige

Prestige Classes are distinction outcomes — never sold.

### A9. Plan level does not multiply Evidence quality

Access Plan tier must not multiply, boost, or otherwise alter Evidence quality scores or approval probability. Plan may affect capacity/entitlement only.

### A10. Community popularity does not create Mastery

Likes, followers, reactions, or community reputation must not award Mastery or Route-Proven.

### A11. Mission volume does not automatically create Mastery

Completing many Missions without approved Evidence / assessment pathways must not automatically create Mastery.

### A12. High XP does not prove professional capability

Flight XP must never be treated as proof of professional capability, employment readiness, or Route-Proven.

### A13. High Momentum does not prove long-term maturity

Season Momentum must never be treated as Maturity Rank or long-horizon maturity proof.

### A14. Route completion does not automatically create Route-Proven

Completing a Route’s Missions/Stages is not Route-Proven. Route-Proven follows the qualitative Learning standard (Evidence, assessments, Capstone, integrity, remediation, Trust conditions as defined).

### A15. Route-Proven in one Route does not create Horizon-Proven

One Route’s Proven standing must never award Horizon-Proven. Horizon-Proven awarding remains deferred per Learning Design Baseline.

### A16. Prestige requires more than activity or longevity

Prestige nomination/award requires governed distinction criteria beyond mere activity volume or account age.

### A17. Trust violations do not erase valid historical learning Evidence

Integrity/Trust sanctions may restrict eligibility and authority; they must not delete or falsify historically valid approved Evidence records. Validity may be marked, sealed, or context-limited — not silently erased as if never learned.

### A18. Evidence revocation may affect current Mastery standing

When Evidence is revoked, current Mastery / Route-Proven / dependent Titles/Prestige eligibility may reverse or suspend. Historical Flight Log may retain the revocation history.

### A19. Temporary inactivity does not erase lifetime XP or approved Evidence

A period of inactivity must not wipe lifetime XP or erase approved Evidence. Momentum season standing may decay/reset by season rules (durations PENDING); that is not lifetime erasure.

> Note: Gate text lists sixteen primary statements plus three Evidence/inactivity statements. This document locks **all** of them as A1–A19 (**19** statements) for unambiguous enforcement.

## B. Mission distinction invariants

Exact total: **10** mission distinctions (from Gate mission statement).

| ID | Invariant | Meaning |
|----|-----------|---------|
| B1 | **Activity ≠ Skill** | Recognized activity (XP and related) is not demonstrated skill. |
| B2 | **Consistency ≠ Mastery** | Momentum consistency is not Mastery. |
| B3 | **Completion ≠ Proof** | Route/Mission completion is not Route-Proven / Evidence proof. |
| B4 | **Popularity ≠ Trust** | Community popularity is not Trust Standing. |
| B5 | **Subscription ≠ Rank** | Access Plan is not Maturity Rank, Momentum League, or Mastery Rank. |
| B6 | **Payment ≠ Prestige** | Money never buys Prestige Class. |
| B7 | **XP ≠ Route-Proven** | Flight XP never substitutes for Route-Proven. |
| B8 | **Route-Proven ≠ Horizon-Proven** | Single-Route Proven never equals Horizon-Proven. |
| B9 | **Professional Title ≠ Employment Guarantee** | Titles are capability bundles, not jobs. |
| B10 | **Prestige ≠ Permanent Immunity** | Prestige does not grant permanent immunity from review, integrity action, or Evidence revalidation. |

## Rules

* Working names are not final.
* No Achievement may be bought.
* Participation Achievements must not resemble Mastery Achievements.
* Return Achievement must not reward deliberate inactivity.
* Repeatable Achievements require explicit caps.
* Revoked source events must revoke or annotate affected Achievements.
* Hidden Achievements cannot be mandatory for progression.
* Artwork remains out of Scope.

## Explainability text

“Achievements mark specific verified moments. They are not for sale and are not the same as Mastery.”

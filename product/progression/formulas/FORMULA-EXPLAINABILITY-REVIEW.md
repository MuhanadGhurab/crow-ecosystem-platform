## Purpose

Provide user-facing explanations for every progression system formula/policy, avoiding mathematical jargon. Every sensitive result must be explainable from source events.

## Flight XP (`FRM-XP-001`)

“Flight XP recognizes validated Missions and related progress. Paying for a plan, logging in, or repeating the same work does not multiply XP.”

## Flight Level (`FRM-LVL-001`)

“Flight Level tracks how much validated activity you have completed over time. It is not a Skill, Maturity, or Mastery grade.”

## Momentum (`FRM-MOM-001` / `FRM-MOM-002`)

“Your Momentum reflects meaningful activity across recent weeks. It does not require a daily streak, and two lower-activity weeks are excluded from your final season result.”

## Maturity (`FRM-MAT-001`)

“Maturity Rank reflects how independently you learn and work across several habits — not how many XP points you have, and not a job title.”

## Mastery (`FRM-MST-001` / `002` / `003`)

“Your Mastery is based on approved Evidence. Strong work cannot replace a missing required capability.”

## Breadth (`FRM-BRD-001`)

“Breadth recognizes distinct demonstrated capabilities and integrated work. Similar skills are not counted repeatedly.”

## Trust (`POL-TRU-001`)

“Trust Standing is a private safety and responsibility state. It is not a public score, and buying a plan cannot improve it.”

## Professional Titles (`TPL-TTL-001` / `002`)

“A Professional Title template describes when a capability recognition may be reviewed. It is not a job title and cannot be bought.”

## Prestige (`FRM-PRS-001` / `POL-PRS-001`)

“Meeting the eligibility threshold opens a human review. It does not automatically grant Prestige.”

## Achievements (`POL-ACH-001`)

“Achievements mark specific verified moments. They are not for sale and are not the same as Mastery.”

## Leaderboards (`FRM-LDB-001`…`006` / `POL-POP-001`)

“Each board measures one kind of standing. Ranked public boards only appear when enough eligible people have opted in.”

## Correction (`POL-COR-001`)

“If a result was based on something later reversed, we correct only the systems that used that source — and we keep an audit history.”

## Freshness (`POL-FRS-001`)

“Past Evidence stays in your history. When a skill area changes quickly, we may ask for a targeted refresh before a current claim.”

## Purpose

## TPL-TTL-001 — Standard Capability Title Candidate Gate

```text
All required Routes = Route-Proven

All required Capability Mastery Indices ≥ 70

All supporting Capability Mastery Indices ≥ 50

Breadth Index ≥ 40 where the title crosses more than one capability cluster

Trust Standing ≥ NORMAL

Required Evidence freshness acceptable

No unresolved integrity issue

Human review where the title may be externally misunderstood
```

## TPL-TTL-002 — Integrated Capability Title Candidate Gate

```text
Required Routes = Route-Proven

Required capabilities across at least two Horizons ≥ 70

Relevant Cross-Wing = Route-Proven or equivalent integrated Evidence

Breadth Index ≥ 65

Trust Standing ≥ POSITIVE_STANDING

Human review required
```

## Rules

* Titles are GHURAVIA capability recognitions.
* They are not employer job titles.
* They do not promise employment.
* They cannot be purchased.
* The final catalogue remains deferred.

## Explainability text

“A Professional Title template describes when a capability recognition may be reviewed. It is not a job title and cannot be bought.”
